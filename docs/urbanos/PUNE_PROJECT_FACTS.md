## How to read this

- **OFFICIAL**: stated by an official publisher: PIB, MoHUA sanction letters, Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha replies, Maha-Metro, PMRDA, PMC or the PMO.
- **2+ OUTLETS**: no official statement was found, but two or more independent reputable outlets agree.
- **SINGLE**: one non-official source only. Treat it as provisional.
- **CONFLICT**: the sources disagree. The primary value is shown, and the alternatives are listed under it.
- **UNVERIFIED**: the value is `null`, with a note explaining why.
- Every quote was checked by script against the page text as fetched on the retrieval date. Table quotes are PDF table cells joined with `|`.
- `as_of` is the date a value was true according to its source. Lines marked ⚠ **slipped** have a target date that moved.

## Verdict on the example: "MahaMetro's Swargate–Katraj tunnel will run about 28 m below ground, with boring starting in January"

**Mostly right. Medium confidence on the depth and medium-to-high on the timing. The January is January 2027, not 2026. No official Maha-Metro document states either figure.**

- **Depth of about 28 m: corroborated by two outlets, not official.** Hindustan Times (21 Aug 2026) reports: *"The TBMs will operate at a depth of around 28 metres below ground level."* Pune Times Mirror (4 Nov 2026) reports: *"The machines will work about 28 metres below ground level."* Prime Time 24 (4 Nov 2026) uses the same wording. All three are reporters' paraphrases in articles quoting Maha-Metro's director (works), Atul Gadgil. Neither is a direct quote.
  - No Maha-Metro press release gives the figure. Neither does the text of the Revised Final DPR, which gives no rail-level or tunnel-depth numbers. Its 9 boreholes were drilled to 12–30 m below ground level.
  - Read 28 m as an approximate operating depth, not a design rail level. Depth will vary along the line: the DPR caps tunnel gradient at 3%, and the stations are cut-and-cover boxes.
  - Beware a lookalike: Line 1's District Court (Civil Court) station was reported as 28 m deep in a 2019 Hindustan Times article. Maha-Metro's 2026 underground brochure now gives it as 33 m.
- **Boring starting in January: means January 2027, and it is a slipped date.** Pune Times Mirror and Prime Time 24, both dated 4 Nov 2026, say tunnelling is *expected* to start in January, once the Swargate launch shaft is ready.
  - Earlier, Hindustan Times (1 Jun 2026) quoted Gadgil: *"The actual TBM operations will start from September"*, meaning September 2026. The work order itself had targeted a start of works in March 2026.
  - On 6 Nov 2026, Hindustan Times reported that the first TBM was being assembled at the Swargate launching shaft, with the second due by end-November.
  - On 21 Oct 2026, TOI had said excavation would begin "in the next few days", which also did not happen.
  - Treat January 2027 as a target that has already slipped twice.
- **What is official about this project:**
  - 5.46 km, fully underground, twin tunnels totalling 9.7 km. TBM segments are 6.35 m outer and 5.8 m inner diameter; casting started 12 May 2026. Five stations: Market Yard, Bibwewadi, Padmavati, Balajinagar, Katraj. Source: Maha-Metro press release.
  - Sanctioned cost ₹2,954.53 crore, with completion due Feb 2029. Source: PIB, 16 Aug 2024.
  - Physical progress 6.2% as of June 2026. Source: Lok Sabha reply, 30 Jul 2026.
- **The completion target has slipped:** Feb 2029 (Cabinet) → Dec 2029 (HT, Jun 2026) → Dec 2030 start of services (Maha-Metro's MD, per Pune Times Mirror, Sep 2026). Maha-Metro's own Feb 2026 brochure says Sept 2028.

## Limitations

- These official pages could not be fetched by script, so their values come from news reports: PMC's riverfront page, PMC's Sinhagad Road flyover page, and MSRDC's site.
- The PMC PARMM page was read through a fallback fetcher. Its cached text is a transcription of that fetch.
- Maha-Metro's underground brochure is image-only. It was read by OCR and checked visually.
- No official station depths exist for Line 1B. No Line 4/4A station list was found on the official page.
- The Katraj flyover's cost and length, and HCMTR's length, are unresolved.
- Ring-road lengths and costs vary by source and by revision. The JSON keeps every variant.
- Values are as of the retrieval date. Statuses change quickly. Line 3 had 17 of 23 stations open on 19 Nov 2026.

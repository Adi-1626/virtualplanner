## Control inventory

Test set-up: built app (`vite build`) served statically, backend on a copy of `backend/twincity.db` (20,732 objects, of which 18,271 are OSM roads), Chromium headless at 1440×900. Every step was a real mouse click or key press on the map or panel (Playwright `page.mouse`), at real Pune places. Each result was read back three ways: the engine's object, `GET /api/objects`, and the city traffic graph (`engineInstance.getTrafficNetwork()`). The base imagery was switched to OpenStreetMap through the base-layer picker, because the sandbox's ion token is refused (HTTP 401).

Known limits seen while testing:
- The test database holds 1,324 saved study areas, most of them overlapping. Their fill (alpha 0.12 each, stacked) paints the map solid blue wherever they are (see the imagery screenshots). The restyle belongs to the study-area work and is not in this branch.
- Without base imagery, demand-zone labels (Wakad, Baner, Aundh…) were drawn over Karve Road, 10 km from their zones. Seen in `tools-noimagery-road-building.png`. Not investigated (zones belong to the design-tools-2 work).
- A full city-graph rebuild takes 1–2 minutes on this machine, so the graph lags an edit by that long. It now catches up after at most one follow-up rebuild, but it is not instant.
- Emoji status icons in the flyover card render as boxes in the headless browser (no emoji font). They are fine on a normal desktop.
